Project & Consulting

We work without selling equipment. Sometimes the most valuable contribution is not the project drawn, but the question asked.

Who This Service Is For

Who benefits from an independent engineering opinion?

  • → Investors and financiers planning greenhouse or large-scale agricultural investments
  • → Businesses that will carry out the work with their own team and want an engineer to draw up the project
  • → Corporate buyers and public institutions that need to prepare specifications and tender documents
  • → Anyone who wants to have a quote/proposal they hold evaluated with an independent eye
  • → Producers applying to government support programs
Independent Quote/Proposal Evaluation

You have a quote/proposal, but you're not sure which one is correct.

You have one or more quotes and are not sure which is correct. This is the most expensive moment of uncertainty in an irrigation investment.

We evaluate the quote against your land, water, and production plan, and share the result in writing. This service does not create an obligation to purchase from us — it would have no value if it lacked independence.

Evaluation scope

  • ✓ Is the proposed system suitable for your land?
  • ✓ Do the number of zones and pipe diameters match the calculations?
  • ✓ Was filtration selected based on water analysis?
  • ✓ Is the pump operating point correct?
  • ✓ What items are not visible in the quote but will be needed?
  • ✓ Is the pricing consistent with the scope?
  • ✓ Are warranty, commissioning, and training included in the scope?
Investment Feasibility

Questions to answer before making equipment decisions

In irrigation and greenhouse investments, there are questions that must be answered before equipment decisions: Can the water source handle this scale? What production plan makes the investment size meaningful? Where do operating costs — energy, fertilizer, labour — land? We prepare reports in a format that can be presented to investors and financial institutions.

Technical Project & Specification

If someone else will carry out the work, you must have a binding technical document in hand.

Scaled layout plan, hydraulic calculation, control unit diagram, line-by-line quantities, and brand-independent technical specification.

It is important that the specification is brand-independent: a specification that describes a specific product closes competition and drives prices up.

Tender & Institutional File Support

Full file support for public institutions and corporate buyers.

Technical specification preparation, site survey and quantities, technical annexes to administrative documents, technical supervision of contractor works, and technical control of payment certificate files.

Government Support Programs

We prepare the technical sections for grant applications.

Technical project, site survey summary, quantities, and justification report for grant and subsidy programs for agricultural irrigation investments.

Training

Hands-on training for the operations team.

System operation, filter and maintenance routines, fertilizer preparation and safety, automation usage, recognizing fault symptoms.

Independence Guarantee

Why do we work without selling equipment?

An engineering firm's recommendation loses its value if it is tied to what the firm gains from that recommendation.

Having a dedicated project and consulting line of business is the guarantee that we apply the same standards even in projects where we sell equipment.
