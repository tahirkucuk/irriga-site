The Heart of Fertigation: ITC Fertiliser Dosing Pumps

Take irrigation efficiency to the next level with ISO-certified precision fertilisation technology exported from Spain to the world.

Partner Spotlight August 2026

The effectiveness of a drip irrigation system is measured not only in water delivery but also in the accuracy of the nutrients delivered with that water. Fertigation — the application of fertilisers and plant nutrients through the irrigation water — is one of the cornerstones of modern agriculture. The heart of this process is the dosing pump. The dosing pump brand we use and recommend at Irriga is ITC, the world-leading Spanish manufacturer.

⚗️ Who Is ITC?

ITC (Innovació Tecnològica Catalana) is a world-leading manufacturer of fertiliser dosing pumps and fertigation equipment, founded in Barcelona, Spain. ITC exports to more than 60 countries and serves the agriculture, water treatment, and industrial process sectors. Part of the Verder Group, the company holds ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015 certifications.

Why Is Fertigation So Important?

Traditional fertilisation relies on applying fertiliser to the field by hand or with a spreader. With this method fertiliser is not distributed uniformly, much of it is left on the surface by rain or irrigation water and is lost before it can leach into the soil, and it takes time to reach the root zone.

In fertigation, fertiliser is mixed in diluted form directly into the irrigation water and delivered precisely to the root zone via emitters. The result: less fertiliser consumption, greater crop uptake, cleaner groundwater, and higher yields.

Integrating an ITC Pump into an Irrigation System

In a fertigation system installed by Irriga, the ITC pump is typically positioned between the fertiliser tank and the main irrigation line. The pump activates in synchronisation with the irrigation programme and doses the specified fertiliser concentration (EC value) with precision. EC and pH sensors continuously verify that the dosing remains within the desired range.

Thanks to this integration, the farmer can:

  • Record how much fertiliser is applied in every irrigation cycle
  • Define different fertiliser programmes for different plots
  • Receive instant alerts in the event of a pump fault or dosing deviation
  • Optimise annual fertiliser consumption by comparing it with historical data

ITC Quality Assurance

ITC pumps are manufactured to be compatible with a broad chemical range, from acidic fertilisers used in agriculture to pH regulators. Parts made from highly chemical-resistant materials such as PVDF extend pump service life and reduce maintenance costs.
