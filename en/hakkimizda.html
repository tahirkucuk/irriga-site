The right project, the right material, the right installation.

We bring irrigation, fertilization and automation together under a single engineering discipline.

Most irrigation investments in Turkey start with the wrong question: "Which dripper should we buy?"

The right question is this: On this land, with this water source, for this crop — how much water, when, by which method and with which fertilizer solution do I need to apply?

Every piece of equipment chosen before this question is answered is a guess. And the cost of guessing usually appears not in the first season but in the third year: clogged drippers, uneven pressure, salt accumulation in the root zone, yields below expectations.

Irriga Engineering was founded to answer this question.

What We Do

Three disciplines under one roof

Irrigation, fertilization and automation are not separate jobs, but three faces of the same system.

💧

Irrigation

Hydraulic calculation, system selection, pipe and pump design, pressure management, filtration.

🌱

Fertilization

Fertigation architecture, dosing, EC/pH control, solution compatibility and mixing plan.

📡

Automation

Field control, soil moisture and climate measurement, satellite imagery, data-driven irrigation decisions.

A filter is not selected without knowing the fertilization plan; valve zoning is not done without knowing the automation scenario. In the industry, these three are usually designed by different companies, unaware of each other. We see the results in the field.

How We Work

The three words in the title mean three separate jobs.

The right project — calculation first, equipment later

An irrigation project starts not with equipment selection but with measurement: water analysis, soil, topography, crop water requirement. We make the equipment selection at the end of the design, not at the beginning.

Every proposal includes a Design Basis Summary: the accepted water requirement, peak flow, number of zones, filtration selection rationale and fertilization approach.

The right material — the rationale for the choice is written down

We know the product range of the solution providers we work with closely: which material is suitable for your land, which control unit gives the right answer at which scale.

This knowledge determines the choice, not what we happen to have on hand. If a more economical solution is sufficient for a project, we do not recommend the expensive one.

The right installation — delivered with measurement

A system is considered delivered not the moment it delivers water but the moment it is verified: lines are flushed, pressure testing is performed, dripper flow is measured, the result is compared against the calculation.

Scope, duration, warranty and price are written down. After delivery, we leave a maintenance program in writing.
