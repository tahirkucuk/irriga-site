Irrigation System Installation Cost and Financing (2026)

For agricultural operations and greenhouse producers, an irrigation system is one of the most critical items in the investment budget. A well-planned drip or sprinkler system both increases yield by 25–40% and cuts water and energy costs in half. So how much does installing an irrigation system cost under 2026 market conditions, and what grants can you benefit from?

1. 2026 Average Installation Costs

The cost per decare (1,000 m²) of irrigation systems varies depending on the selected method, land structure and filtration/automation level. The table below summarises average budget ranges for turnkey projects across Turkey:

System Type Suitable Area Average Cost per Decare (da)
Open-field drip irrigation Corn, cotton, sunflower, sugar beet 12,000 – 25,000 ₺ / da
Orchard drip irrigation Olive, walnut, apple, citrus 18,000 – 35,000 ₺ / da
Greenhouse drip irrigation (pressurised) Tomato, cucumber, pepper, strawberry 30,000 – 65,000 ₺ / da
Micro-sprinkler & sprinkler Nursery, vineyard, fodder crops 20,000 – 45,000 ₺ / da
IoT + Smart Automation Add-on Operations of any scale + 15,000 – 60,000 ₺ / project
💡 Note:

The figures above are rough market-average estimates; precise pricing is prepared after a site survey, topographic slope assessment, water analysis and flow calculation. Drilling and main pump station infrastructure are evaluated separately.

2. 4 Critical Factors That Determine Cost

1. Land Area and Plot Geometry

The unit cost per decare drops noticeably on large, regular plots. For small or highly fragmented land under 5 decares, fixed investment items such as the filter unit, main valves and control equipment increase the cost per decare.

2. Type and Distance of Water Source

If there is already an active deep well or gravity-fed canal water on the land, the investment consists only of the distribution line. If new drilling is required from scratch, additional infrastructure costs must be planned depending on depth and ground hardness.

3. Equipment Standard and Pipe Quality

Italian or Spanish pressure-compensating emitters may appear slightly more expensive at initial investment, but their resistance to clogging and service life exceeding 10 years always pay off in total cost of ownership. Poor-quality pipes can crack and leak in the first season, causing crop losses.

4. Automation and Sensor Integration

There is a wide range of options, from manually operated valves to cloud-based smart irrigation platforms like WiseConn DropControl. While automation increases the initial investment, it eliminates the need for labour and prevents fertiliser loss by avoiding overwatering.

3. Government Grants and Interest-Free Credit Options

KOSGEB Grants

Corporate enterprises engaged in agricultural production and SMEs can benefit from low-interest or interest-subsidised financing packages for irrigation automation investments under the scope of technology modernisation.

TKDK IPARD Rural Development Grant Programme

In IPARD-covered provinces across Turkey, TKDK offers non-repayable grants of up to 50% for pressurised irrigation systems and modernisation projects. An engineering project and technical specification are required at the time of application.

📋 TKDK Application Note:

Grant calls open on specific schedules. Having water analysis, soil survey and hydraulic project files ready before the call is announced multiplies the chances of acceptance. Irriga Engineering provides full support in preparing the technical application file.

Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry Individual Irrigation Grant Support

The Ministry provides 50% grant support for drip and sprinkler irrigation machinery/equipment purchases through its annual circular. Every producer registered in the Farmer Registration System (FRS) can apply.

Ziraat Bank Interest-Subsidised Irrigation Credit

Treasury-backed, interest-reduced subsidised agricultural loans with terms of up to 7 years are allocated to producers installing pressurised drip irrigation systems.

4. Repayment Calculation: A Realistic Case Study

Let us take a 14-decare protected strawberry greenhouse as an example. Monthly water consumption under the old irrigation method was 280 m³, while after installing a hydraulically balanced drip irrigation and fertigation system, consumption dropped to 185 m³:

  • System Installation Cost (14 da): ~450,000 ₺
  • Annual Water and Electricity Savings: ~65,000 ₺
  • Labour Time Savings: ~40,000 ₺
  • Yield Increase from Uniform Fertilisation: ~30,000 ₺
  • Total Annual Net Contribution: ~135,000 ₺
  • Payback Period (Without Grant): ~3.3 Years
  • Payback with TKDK 50% Grant: ~1.7 Years

5. Conclusion: How Should the Investment Decision Be Made?

Three fundamental rules to follow for a successful irrigation system project:

  1. Site Survey and Hydraulic Calculation: Template offers prepared without measuring soil structure, elevation difference and flow rate cause problems in the field.
  2. Research Grant and Credit Eligibility in Advance: You may be able to cover half of your investment budget from government sources.
  3. Seek an Engineering Guarantee: Lock the pipe wall thickness, emitter spacing and warranty commitment into the contract.
