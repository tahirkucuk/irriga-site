The award-winning platform active on over 500,000 acres worldwide — turning your data into irrigation, and irrigation into data.

Partner Spotlight August 2026

Installing an irrigation system is the starting point; running it at the right time, in the right amount, and in the right place is where the real value is created. When we add an automation layer to the systems we install at Irriga, the platform we turn to is WiseConn DropControl, trusted by thousands of farmers around the world.

📡 Who Is WiseConn?

WiseConn is one of the global leaders in precision irrigation automation. The DropControl platform — combining IoT-based field hardware, cloud software, and a mobile app — gives farmers full control of their fields from a computer or smartphone. It operates with more than 20,000 node installations worldwide and a network of over 50 authorised distributors.

How Does the DropControl Platform Work?

The WiseConn system consists of three layers: hardware installed in the field (nodes and valves), the communication infrastructure connecting them to the cloud, and the DropControl software that visualises all data. Farmers manage this system from both a web browser and a mobile app.

  1. Field Data Collection RF-M1 monitoring nodes collect soil moisture sensor, weather station, and meter data every few minutes and transmit it to the cloud.
  2. Automated Decision & Scheduling DropControl runs the irrigation programme based on soil moisture and weather data. When moisture falls below a defined threshold, the system activates automatically.
  3. Remote Valve Control Via the RF-X1 controller, you can open and close each valve individually and set duration and flow rate — all from your phone without visiting the field.
  4. Reporting & Analysis Water consumption, irrigation history, sensor graphs, and alert logs are automatically recorded on the platform. Detailed reports can be downloaded at the end of the season.

WiseConn Hardware Family

RF-X1
Full Automation Controller

The main control unit that opens and closes irrigation valves on a schedule. Manages multiple zones independently. Compatible with fertigation integration.

RF-M1
Monitoring Node

Data collection unit that connects soil moisture sensors, weather stations, and meters to the system. Requires no electrical connection — can run on solar power.

RF-C1
Smart Irrigation Controller

Compact control unit for small and medium-scale plots. Fully integrated with DropControl, quick to install.

RF-V1
Wireless Valve Controller

Unit that wirelessly controls valves in remote plots where cable routing is difficult. Operates on battery or solar power.

NDVI Satellite Analysis Integration

The DropControl platform also integrates satellite-based NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) imaging. The plot's plant health map is updated periodically, and areas with high irrigation needs are detected automatically. This feature completely eliminates the need for manual observation on large operations of 50 acres and above.

Irriga — WiseConn Integration

When we integrate WiseConn hardware into the drip irrigation and sprinkler irrigation systems we install at Irriga, what we deliver to our customers includes:

  • 24/7 irrigation control from a phone or computer
  • Automatic irrigation decisions supported by soil moisture sensor data
  • Real-time and monthly consumption tracking via water meter integration
  • Automatic irrigation cancellation before rain based on weather forecast data
  • SMS/email alerts in the event of a fault, low pressure, or communication loss
  • Season-based irrigation reports and historical data comparison

We source this technology through Smart Soil, WiseConn's Turkish distributor, and can integrate it into all our projects in the Aegean region and surrounding provinces. System installation, parameter configuration, and user training are carried out on-site by the Irriga team.
