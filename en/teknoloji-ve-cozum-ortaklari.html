Components & Solution Partners

Selection criteria across five key components

We ask the same question in every component: which option is the best fit for this project?

Water Conveyance & Distribution

Pipes, laterals and emitters

Solution Partner

Agrodrip

Since 1989, 40+ countries

What we look for in emitter selection: flow rate deviation (CV), anti-drip feature, clog resistance and pressure compensation. In lateral pipe selection: wall thickness, UV resistance and joint-point sealing. In mainline selection: working pressure and DN calculation. Agrodrip is a supplier with accessible technical data and field-tested performance.

Filtration

Sand, disc and screen filters

Approach

Brand-independent selection

Determined by water quality

Filter type is determined by water quality: sand filter for sand and organic matter, disc filter for fine particles, screen filter under low contamination loads. Sizing is based on peak flow rate, filtration degree and backwash frequency. Rather than a specific brand, we recommend the most suitable technical solution based on the project's water analysis results.

Fertilization & Dosing

Fertigation equipment

Solution Partner

ITC

Innovació Tecnològica Catalana, Spain

In dosing pump selection: proportional precision, multi-channel operating capacity and integration with the control system. ITC stands out with a wide product range in hydraulic and electric dosing pumps. The injection method (venturi, hydraulic, electric) is separately evaluated based on project requirements.

Automation & Control

Control platform

Solution Partner

WiseConn

DropControl platform, Chile

WiseConn DropControl provides multi-point valve management, flow monitoring, alarm system and ET-based decision support. Its cloud-based architecture allows mobile and web access. Conventional control units are also used in small-scale systems; the level of automation and cost balance are evaluated on a project basis.

Measurement & Monitoring

Soil moisture sensors

Solution Partner

Sentek (Australia)

Turkey: via Smartsoil

Sentek capacitance sensors offer multi-depth profile measurement, high resolution and precise calibration. Sales and technical support in Turkey are provided through Smartsoil. Sensor type and depth configuration are determined according to soil structure and crop type; the soil profile is evaluated prior to installation.

Brand Independence

Why are we not tied to specific brands?

The long-term suppliers we work with are listed above. However, every project is different. The best choice in one project may not be the right choice in another.

We base component selection on technical criteria — water quality, pressure conditions, flow requirements, crop type and automation targets. We select to make the project work correctly, not to sell a particular product.

The equipment to be used in the project and the rationale behind each choice are shared with you at the start of the design process.
