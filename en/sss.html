Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about irrigation systems.

🌱 General
Yes, the initial site survey visit is completely free. Our team visits your land in person, evaluates your needs on-site, and presents a detailed price quote within 48 hours.
We actively serve all districts in our region and neighbouring provinces. For large-scale projects we can reach a wider area. When you tell us your location we can give you precise information.
No, we have no minimum area requirement. We serve every scale, from small gardens to large field projects exceeding 100 decares.
The most suitable system is determined by land size, plant type, water source, and budget. At the site survey visit our specialists will explain the most efficient option in detail. Drip irrigation is preferred for precise, water-saving applications, while sprinkler irrigation is preferred for uniform coverage over large areas.
🔧 Installation Process
It varies by land size and system type. For an average 5–10 decare field, 3–5 working days is sufficient; for greenhouse projects, 5–8 days. For large-scale projects, the timeline is planned per project.
After quote approval and deposit are received, materials are sourced and planning is done. Installation typically starts within 5–10 working days. This may vary depending on seasonal workload; we confirm the date at the quote stage.
Being present at the start and end of installation is sufficient. In between, our team works independently and keeps you informed about progress via WhatsApp.
Yes, in our turnkey service we handle all material procurement. We source high-quality, long-lasting products from reliable suppliers. A material list can be added to the quote on request.
⚙️ Technical Topics
We design and install irrigation systems for all agricultural products including tomatoes, peppers, strawberries, corn, potatoes, citrus, olives, vineyards, fruit orchards, hazelnuts, and greenhouse crops.
We can provide guidance on drilling a well or installing a storage tank. We design your system integrated with mains water, a well, a pond, or a storage source.
Yes. With the automation and remote monitoring package you can start, stop, and schedule the system via a smartphone app. With moisture sensor integration, irrigation can become fully automatic.
With quality materials and proper installation, main lines can last 15–20 years, and drip laterals and sprinkler heads 8–12 years. Regular maintenance and correct end-of-season preparation extend system life.
💰 Cost & Payment
Land size, land topography, irrigation type (drip / sprinkler / sprinkler irrigation), water pressure and distance, automation requirements, and the quality of materials to be used are the main factors determining the price.
50% deposit after quote approval, and the remaining 50% when installation is complete and the system is operational. A payment plan can be discussed for large projects. Bank transfer / EFT is accepted.
Yes, we issue e-invoices / e-archive invoices for all transactions. Proforma invoices can also be issued for corporate customers.
🛡️ Maintenance & Warranty
Yes. 1-year workmanship warranty for installation, and the supplier warranty applies for the main materials we use. We provide on-site intervention for faults covered by the warranty.
Yes. We offer an annual maintenance package including pre-season system check, filter cleaning, pipe integrity inspection, and scheduling updates. Maintenance contract customers receive priority fault response.
When you report via our WhatsApp line we respond as quickly as possible. During the irrigation season we generally respond to critical faults the same day or the next day.
In areas with frost risk, it is recommended to properly drain and shut down the system at the end of the season. We inform you about this during installation, and we also provide a shutdown service on request.
