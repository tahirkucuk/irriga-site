Turkey's Representative for Precision Agriculture: Smart Soil

Comprehensive technology solutions spanning soil moisture, drone analysis, irrigation automation, and weather stations.

Partner Spotlight August 2026

At Irriga Irrigation Systems, our goal in every irrigation project we install is to deliver not just on-site assembly and pipe laying, but data-driven, intelligent, and sustainable agricultural solutions as a complete package. The most important partner in achieving this goal is Smart Soil, Turkey's authorised address for precision agriculture technologies.

🌱 Who Is Smart Soil?

Smart Soil is the authorised Turkish distributor of Sentek soil moisture sensors, the WiseConn irrigation automation platform, and MicaSense multispectral drone cameras. It is a company founded with the mission of bringing precision agriculture technologies to Turkish farmers, operating with a local technical service and support network.

Sentek Soil Moisture Sensors: Base Your Irrigation on Data

In traditional irrigation methods, farmers irrigate based on observation and experience. However, this approach leads to both water waste and yield losses from under- or over-irrigation. Sentek soil moisture sensors solve this problem at its root.

Installed at specific depths in the soil, these sensors measure root-zone moisture percentage, electrical conductivity (salinity), and soil temperature every 15 minutes and transmit the data to the cloud. Through the app, the farmer sees clear decisions such as "current soil moisture 28%, critical threshold 22%, irrigation not required." This makes irrigation timing and quantity entirely data-driven.

WiseConn Integration: Field Hardware and Software Together

Smart Soil's WiseConn distributorship directly complements Irriga projects. When we integrate WiseConn RF series controllers into the drip irrigation systems we install, the farmer can, from their phone without visiting the field:

  • Irrigate any zone manually or on a schedule
  • Monitor soil moisture sensor data in real time
  • Track electricity and water consumption
  • View irrigation history and reports

MicaSense Drone Cameras: Analyse Your Field From Above

On large agricultural operations it is impossible to see by eye which plot is under water stress or how plant health is changing. MicaSense RedEdge-P and ALTUM-PT multispectral cameras, mounted on a drone, generate NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) maps of the field. Irrigation deficiencies, signs of disease, or fertilisation shortfalls are detected at an early stage.

What Does the Irriga — Smart Soil Partnership Mean?

At Irriga, while we install the physical infrastructure (pipes, emitters, pumps, filters), we integrate Smart Soil technologies into that infrastructure. The result: our customers receive not merely "a system that carries water" but a data-connected, remotely manageable, self-optimising irrigation solution.

This partnership delivers significant cost advantages and yield improvements particularly for large field operations, orchards, and greenhouse projects. In projects supported by soil moisture sensors, our customers report average water savings of 25–35%.
